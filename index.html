CASE STUDY 01 ILLUSTRATIVE REAL ESTATE GROWTH SCENARIO

Turning property enquiries into a connected growth system.

A real estate business receives enquiries through paid campaigns, website forms and social media, but lead management is fragmented, sales follow-up is inconsistent and marketing performance is difficult to measure.

THE CHALLENGE

Enquiry sources, sales activities and marketing data are disconnected. Potential buyers, sellers and renters risk being overlooked due to delayed follow-ups, unstructured lead qualification and limited visibility into the conversion journey.

THE APPROACH
  • Structure Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns around buyer, seller and rental intent.
  • Improve lead capture through focused landing pages, enquiry forms and conversion tracking.
  • Configure HubSpot CRM with structured property enquiry stages and lead-source tracking.
  • Automate lead acknowledgement, assignment, follow-up reminders and prospect nurturing.
HOW SUCCESS IS MEASURED

Total enquiries · Qualified leads · Property viewings booked · Lead-to-viewing rate · Cost per qualified enquiry · Lead response time · Channel performance · Pipeline progression
